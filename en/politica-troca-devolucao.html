Returns and exchange policy

Purchases on the official Sensor CrashFix store (website). Marketplace orders follow each platform’s rules.

Withdrawal (7 days)

For website orders you may cancel within 7 calendar days of delivery. The product must be unused, in the original packaging, with kit accessories included.

Manufacturing defect

There is a legal warranty against manufacturing defects. If the lens or kit has a manufacturing fault, contact us with your order number and photos — we will guide exchange or replacement as appropriate.

What is not covered

Incorrect use, installation outside the kit manual, damage after poor alignment, or trying to reuse a lens that was already applied are not manufacturing defects.

How to request

Email contato@sensorcrashfix.com.br with: order number, reason (withdrawal or defect), and photos when relevant. We reply with the return or exchange steps.
