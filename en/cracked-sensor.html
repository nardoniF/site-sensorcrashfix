What happens when the sensor cracks

The sensor glass on the back of the watch seals the case and passes light for optical HR. When it cracks or breaks:

Seal is lost

Water, sweat and moisture get in — the watch is no longer water-resistant as before.

Unstable readings

Heart rate, SpO₂ and wrist detection fail or drop mid-workout.

Crack gets worse

Without a cover, impacts and dirt widen the crack and expose the module.

How the Sensor CrashFix lens helps

It is an adhesive optical cover that goes on top of the cracked or broken sensor glass. No opening the watch, no module swap. In many cases it:

  • Restores the seal over the damaged area
  • Stabilizes optical reading again (wrist and heart rate)
  • Protects the glass so the crack does not spread

Works with Apple Watch, Samsung, Garmin and more

Designed for the rear optical sensor on major smartwatches and bands. Check size on the product page before you buy — the right fit is what restores the seal.

When it is worth it (and when it is not)

Best when the sensor glass is cracked or broken and the watch still powers on. If the internal module is dead (no LEDs, board damage), a lens will not replace hardware repair. For most cracked-glass cases with failing HR, it is the simplest step before an expensive module swap.

FAQ: cracked or broken sensor

Can I still use a watch with a cracked sensor?

Often yes — but without a proper seal and with unstable readings. The lens covers the damage so daily use is safer again.

Is a broken sensor the same as cracked?

Cracked means a fissure in the glass. Broken may mean missing pieces or shattered glass. The lens can cover the sensor area if there is still a surface to adhere to.

Does the lens restore waterproofing?

It reseals the sensor zone. It is not a factory IP rating — the goal is to seal the damage for normal use (hands, sweat, light rain).

Do I need to replace the sensor module?

Usually not for cracked glass. CrashFix is a cover — far cheaper than parts plus labour.
