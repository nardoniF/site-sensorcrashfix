The Problem

A crack in the sensor can look “cosmetic only”—until the area is exposed. Dirt, sweat and splashes enter through the fissure; optical reading becomes unstable; the watch is less reliable day to day. Without protection, the crack can get worse.

Exposed area

With cracked glass, protection against dirt and splashes around the sensor is compromised.

Vulnerable sensor

The crack leaves the optical sensor more exposed to impact, sweat and dirt.

Unstable reading

The crack interferes with the optical interface—heart-rate reading may fail.

Be careful with water

After damage, avoid submerging the watch until you assess the sensor (service or a cover).

Expensive repair

Official module/sensor replacement is costly and slow—many prefer an immediate cover.

Risk of getting worse

Without protection, the crack can grow—and sensor damage gets worse.

Why quick fixes fail

After a crack, many people improvise—and make it worse:

  • Tape and generic stickers: They peel with sweat; cover poorly and dirty the sensor.
  • DIY glue / resin: Can push moisture inside, leave residue and make a future repair harder.
  • Ignoring the crack: Damage tends to grow and optical reading gets worse with use.

How to apply

Step by step from the kit manual. Follow the order—cleaning matters as much as alignment.

  1. Clean the sensor with the kit wipe until oil and dust are gone.
  2. Peel the film from the lens adhesive.
  3. Align the lens on the sensor center (diameter sized for your model).
  4. Press about 30 seconds with the applicator, without edge bubbles.

More detail in the printed kit manual. Demo channel: YouTube @Sensorcrashfix.

When NOT to use the kit

CrashFix covers the sensor glass. In these cases, seek service—the kit does not replace structural repair.

  • Module broken beyond the glass—damaged internals or a displaced sensor.
  • Loose parts—back cover, screws or rattling components.
  • Moisture already inside the watch—internal fogging, failures after getting wet, corrosion.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the lens compatible with my smartwatch? Which size?

Yes, for watches and bands with a rear optical sensor. What changes is the lens diameter (mm)—pick your model/family in the store (Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch, Garmin, Amazfit and others).

Does the lens interfere with charging?

No. It’s thin and compatible with inductive charging; use the original charger with the lens on.

How long does the lens last?

Depends on use and careful application. Normally it stays adhered; if it lifts or scratches, replace with the correct size.

What warranty do I get?

Statutory consumer product warranty. For exchanges or manufacturing defects, contact us by email.

Can I get the watch wet with the lens on?

The lens helps protect the cracked area from dirt and splashes; submersion is not recommended. Without a water-resistance test of the applied kit, treat light sweat/rain carefully and avoid swimming or bathing with the watch.

Is it different from TattooFix?

Yes. CrashFix is for a cracked/broken sensor (enlarged cover over the fissure). If your case is different—for example the watch keeps asking for the passcode—see Sensor TattooFix.

Does the kit replace the sensor module?

No. It covers and protects the cracked area on top of the glass. It does not open the watch or replace internal parts.

How do I install it?

Clean the sensor (kit wipe), peel the adhesive film, align the lens in the center and press for about 30 seconds. Details in How to apply.
