The Problem

A crack in the sensor looks “only cosmetic” — until the seal fails. Water, sweat and moisture get in; optical reading becomes unstable; the watch stops being trustworthy day to day. And without protection, the crack can get worse.

Lost Seal

With cracked glass, the smartwatch is no longer water-resistant as before.

Exposed Sensor

The crack leaves the optical sensor vulnerable to impact, sweat and dirt.

Unstable Heart Rate

The crack interferes with the optical interface — pulse reading can fail.

Afraid of Water

Rain, washing hands or light swimming become a risk after sensor damage.

Expensive Repair

Official sensor module replacement is costly and slow — many prefer an immediate cover.

Risk of further damage

Without protection, the crack can grow — and sensor damage gets even worse.

Why DIY Fixes Fail

After a crack, many people improvise — and make it worse:

  • Tape and generic stickers: Peel off with sweat and water; they don’t truly seal and dirty the sensor.
  • Home glue / resin: Can push moisture inside, leave residue and complicate a future repair.
  • “Keep it dry forever”: You give up training, rain and routine — the watch stops doing its job and can get damaged further.

About Us

Sensor Crash Fix was born from Sensor Tattoo Fix — the product made to restore smartwatch functions on tattooed skin.

Smartwatch optical sensors often don’t read well on tattooed skin. Sensor Tattoo Fix developed a graded lens, with model-specific material, that restores watch functions in that case.

Some people started buying the Tattoo Fix lens even without tattoos — for damaged / cracked sensors. In many cases the sensor worked again and the watch became water-resistant once more.

That’s how Crash Fix began

So we created Sensor Crash Fix: a “cousin” of Tattoo Fix — not only to restore reading on a cracked sensor, but also to restore the seal of an expensive watch.

What changes vs the Tattoo Fix lens? The Crash Fix lens is larger: it extends past the sensor glass so it can seal beyond the rim — for cracks or breaks that run all the way to the edge of the sensor. Tattoo Fix covers the reading area; Crash Fix needs to go further to reseal those edge-to-edge cracks.
