The Problem

A crack in the sensor looks “only cosmetic” — until the seal fails. Water, sweat and moisture get in; optical reading becomes unstable; the watch stops being trustworthy day to day.

Lost Seal

With cracked glass, the smartwatch is no longer water-resistant as before.

Exposed Sensor

The crack leaves the optical sensor vulnerable to impact, sweat and dirt.

Unstable Heart Rate

The crack interferes with the optical interface — pulse reading can fail.

Afraid of Water

Rain, washing hands or light swimming become a risk after sensor damage.

Expensive Repair

Official sensor module replacement is costly and slow — many prefer an immediate cover.

Watch on the Shelf

Without protection, an expensive device sits unused for fear of making it worse.

Why DIY Fixes Fail

After a crack, many people improvise — and make it worse:

  • Tape and generic stickers: Peel off with sweat and water; they don’t truly seal and dirty the sensor.
  • Home glue / resin: Can push moisture inside, leave residue and complicate a future repair.
  • “Keep it dry forever”: You give up training, rain and routine — the watch stops being useful.

About Us

Sensor Crash Fix was born from a real problem: a smartwatch with a cracked sensor.

3N20 already built optical wearable solutions. We kept seeing the same pattern: a crack in the sensor glass, compromised seal, fear of water — and an expensive watch stuck in a drawer.

It wasn’t “only cosmetic.” With a cracked sensor, protection fails. Sweat, rain and washing hands become a risk. Heart-rate reading often becomes unstable too.

The solution

We developed a water-resistant optical cover lens in slightly larger diameters to better cover the damaged area. Clear priorities: restore the seal, protect the cracked sensor, and recover optical reading when the crack interfered.

That’s how Sensor Crash Fix was born — same 3N20 lens family, positioned for physical sensor damage.
